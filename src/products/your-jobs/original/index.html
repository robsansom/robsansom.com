Apply

Fill the form in one tap.

Applications open inside Your Jobs, exactly as the employer built them. Assist does the typing, so you’re editing rather than retyping.

  1. Open the application

    The employer’s own form, on their own site, without leaving the app.

  2. Tap Assist

    It finds every empty field it can match with an answer you’ve written, and fills them all at once.

  3. Read it, then press send

    Nothing is ever submitted for you. You check the form and press the employer’s own button.

Automatic filling is part of Pro. Copying your saved answers yourself is always free.

Questions

Good questions.

Anything else? The support page has more, and a real person answers email.

Is Your Jobs free?

Yes. Searching is free and always will be: every job, every filter and every board, plus your saved jobs, your whole application history, your written answers and your CVs, with no cap and no adverts. Pro adds the parts where the app does the work for you — filling forms, writing help and more than one saved search with alerts — for $4.99 a month or $29.99 once. Prices vary by country; Apple shows yours before you buy.

Where do the jobs come from?

Straight from the job boards employers publish themselves — the careers pages companies run on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters and others — plus NHS Jobs in the UK. Over two hundred employers are built in, and you can add any company by typing its name.

Does Your Jobs send applications for me?

No. Assist fills in the fields it can match with answers you’ve written, but nothing is ever submitted for you. You read the form and press the employer’s own button.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no Your Jobs account and no server of ours. Your answers, CVs and applications live on your device and sync between your iPhone and iPad through your own private iCloud.

Can I track jobs I found on LinkedIn or Indeed?

Yes. Share the link from LinkedIn, Indeed, Safari, Mail or Messages to Your Jobs, or paste it in the app, and it’s saved and tracked alongside everything else.

Which countries does Your Jobs cover?

Employers are built in across the United Kingdom, the United States, Germany, France, Spain and Japan, along with remote roles that hire across borders. The app is available in English, German, French, Spanish and Japanese.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone or iPad on iOS or iPadOS 26.2 or later. Writing help uses Apple Intelligence, so it needs a device that supports it; everything else works on every supported device.
