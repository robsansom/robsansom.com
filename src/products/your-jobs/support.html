Frequently asked

A company I want isn’t in the feed. Can I add it?

Yes. Open Profile, then Company boards, and type the company’s name. Your Jobs looks for its job board and, if it finds one, every role it lists appears in your feed from then on. You can also paste a link to any of the company’s job ads. Some employers use recruiting systems that don’t publish a public board, and those can’t be added — but you can still share or paste a single job to track it.

How do I save a job I found on LinkedIn, Indeed or elsewhere?

Tap Share on the job, choose Your Jobs, and pick whether you’re saving it or applying. Or copy the link and use Add from link in the app. It’s then saved and tracked with everything else.

How does Assist fill in a form?

Open the job’s application inside Your Jobs and tap Assist. It matches the empty fields on the page with answers you’ve saved — name, email, phone, links, notice period and so on — and fills them. It never submits anything: you check the form and press the employer’s own button. Filling is part of Pro; copying a saved answer yourself is always free.

Assist missed a field. Why?

Assist only fills fields it can match confidently with an answer you’ve written, and leaves the rest alone. Give the answer a clearer name, or add one for that question, and it will match next time. Some employer forms are built in ways no app can fill — upload widgets and custom dropdowns especially — so a quick look before you send is always worth it.

My alerts aren’t arriving.

Alerts rely on iOS waking the app in the background. Check that notifications are allowed for Your Jobs (Settings → Notifications), that Background App Refresh is on (Settings → General → Background App Refresh), and that Low Power Mode is off — iOS pauses background work while it’s on. Opening the app also refreshes everything straight away.

How do I get my data on my iPad too?

Sign in to the same Apple Account on both devices, with iCloud turned on for Your Jobs. Your applications, saved answers, saved searches and CVs sync through your own private iCloud.

Writing help isn’t showing up.

Writing help runs on Apple Intelligence, on the device. It needs an iPhone or iPad that supports Apple Intelligence, with it turned on in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri. Everything else in Your Jobs works without it.

What’s in Pro, and what does it cost?

Free covers finding the job and keeping your record: every job, filter and board, saved jobs, your whole application history, your saved answers and CVs, and one saved search with its alert. Pro adds the work the app does for you: filling the whole form in one tap, inserting saved answers into fields, writing help, and as many saved searches with alerts as you like. It’s $4.99 a month, or $29.99 once for lifetime. Prices vary by country — Apple shows yours before you buy.

I bought Pro but it isn’t unlocked.

Make sure you’re signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with, then open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Your Jobs and tap Cancel Subscription. Pro stays active until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, find the Your Jobs purchase and request a refund.

How do I delete my data?

Everything you create lives on your devices and in your private iCloud — we don’t hold a copy. Delete records in the app, or remove the app and then delete its data in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
